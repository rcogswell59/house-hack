# Assignment 1 — Product description (DRAFT, rewrite in your own voice before submitting)

HouseHack is a web application that helps first-time homebuyers in Brevard County, Florida find a 2–4 unit property they can live in while tenants in the other units cover most of the mortgage. The problem it solves is that listing sites like Zillow show a property's price, but not what a specific buyer would actually pay each month after rental income, financing, taxes, and insurance, and not which Florida and Brevard County assistance programs that buyer qualifies for. HouseHack collects active multi-family listings every day from a licensed real estate data API, enriches each one with the Brevard County Property Appraiser's public parcel and tax records, and runs it through the buyer's own profile: income, savings, credit, first-time-buyer status, occupation, and commute limit to their workplace near Cape Canaveral. The analyzer models an FHA 3.5%-down loan with the homestead exemption applied only to the owner's unit, checks eligibility for the Florida Hometown Heroes, Brevard Housing Finance Authority, and SHIP down-payment programs, and produces two numbers for every listing: the buyer's net monthly housing cost after rent, and whether the property passes the lender's FHA rental-income tests. It ranks properties by net monthly cost and alerts the user when a new listing enters their top ten. The product is geared toward young professionals buying their first home in Brevard County who want to use house-hacking to live cheaply and build equity. It is unique because no listing site or mortgage calculator combines live listings, county tax data, owner-occupied multi-family financing rules, and location-specific assistance programs into a single personalized ranking; every rule is driven by the user's profile, so the same system extends to other buyers and counties without new code.
