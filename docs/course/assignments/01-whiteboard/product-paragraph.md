# Assignment 1 — Product description (DRAFT, adjust to your own voice before submitting)

I recently accepted a full-time offer near Cape Canaveral, Florida, and I want to buy a small multi-family home: live in one unit and rent out the others. Listing sites like Zillow show the price, but not what I would actually pay each month after rent, taxes, insurance, and the first-time buyer programs I qualify for. HouseHack solves that. Every day it collects 2–4 unit listings and public county parcel data, models my FHA financing and Florida assistance programs, and ranks each property by my true monthly cost. It also shows separately whether a lender would approve the loan. The first prototype is built for one user, me, but everything is driven by a buyer profile (income, savings, commute, location), so the same system can serve any first-time house hacker in any county. Nothing else I've found combines live listings, county records, and personal eligibility rules into one ranking.
